About

Hi, I'm Ruturaj. I'm a physics undergrad, and I started The Nobel Minute back in May with a simple rule: one Physics Nobel Prize a day, picked at random, explained in about two minutes, no skipping, no repeats. By the end, that was the whole history of the prize, one year at a time.

If you want to talk about the series, physics, or anything in between, reach me at thenobelminute@gmail.com or on Instagram.
